# 0.2.1 — 2026-11-20

## Added

- **Earnings calendar and company news** return `nextOffset` when additional fetched results are available. Earnings history and analyst recommendations accept larger limits (#17).
- **Earnings calendar** includes reported EPS and revenue where available and documents `dmh` for reports during market hours (#19).
- **Symbol search** accepts an exchange filter (#18).
- **Company peers** support sub-industry, industry, and sector grouping (#20).

## Changed

- Synced framework tooling and updated the Finnhub tool guide.

## Fixed

- **Earnings calendar and company news** report fetched date bounds and flag possible upstream omissions (#13).
- **Date windows** validate real, inclusive UTC dates and reject ranges whose start follows their end (#14).
- **Symbol search** ranks exact ticker matches first (#15).
- **Financial news** converts headline and summary HTML to plain text (#16).
